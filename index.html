    How each piece is built
    Text: native RCS message, written by the AI agent. Rich card: one image, title, description and up to 4 buttons. Layouts like routes and checklists are images we generate per booking. Buttons: native suggested replies and actions: call, map, calendar, open link, web view. Web view: a secure page we host. Opens inside the thread on Android; plan for the browser on iPhone. No RCS? The same conversation goes out as SMS, with links in place of cards.

    How it works

    More than a chatbot. An agent that follows your rules.

    Most of the operations work follows rules that change with aircraft, destination, passengers and itinerary. The agent pairs conversation with those rules and your booking system, and your team keeps control of exceptions and anything safety-critical.

    Rules engine

    Tropic's requirements

    Weight and baggage limits by aircraft and route, documents by destination, minors, pets, car seats, and the conditions that send a case to a person.

    Conversational AI

    Talks like your best agent

    Understands "I'm 185, the kids are about 70 and 48" and follows up until every requirement is complete, over RCS, SMS, email or voice.

    TakeFlight integration

    Reads and writes back

    Pulls the booking, manifest and itinerary, then writes verified weights, documents and services back where TakeFlight supports it.

    Ops dashboard

    Exceptions, not busywork

    Your team sees what's incomplete, what's waiting on them and why, and approves anything that needs operational judgment.

    The aim is to automate the repetitive majority of passenger coordination while people own the exceptions. How large that majority is at Tropic is something we'd measure together in discovery, not a number we're assuming.

    Controlled pilot

    Start with the work that costs your team the most time.

    1. Missing passenger informationDetect what each booking still needs and follow up until it's complete.
    2. Passport and document remindersSecure collection, checked against the manifest and the destination's requirements.
    3. Passenger weight collectionWeights and bags in plain conversation, flagged against route limits before the day of travel.
    4. Preflight instructionsWhere to go, when to arrive and what to bring, sent at the right time for each itinerary.
    5. Frequently asked questionsCar seats, luggage, pets, customs. Answered from Tropic-approved policy, any hour.
